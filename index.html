    Where to go from Turin

    A few places within a short drive. Check local rules before you pitch: free camping is restricted in much of Italy, so most trips start at a campsite.

    Gran Paradiso

    About 1.5 hours

    Italy’s oldest national park: high valleys, ibex and big alpine views.

    Val di Susa

    About 1 hour

    The easy escape west of the city, with forests, forts and trails.

    Valle Maira

    About 2 hours

    Quiet stone villages and long walking routes in the Cuneo Alps.

    Lago d’Orta

    About 1.5 hours

    A calm lake weekend with swimming spots and lakeside campsites.
